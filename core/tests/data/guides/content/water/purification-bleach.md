# Disinfecting Water with Household Bleach

Use plain, unscented household bleach (sodium hypochlorite 5–9%). Add 2 drops per litre
(8 drops per gallon) of clear water, stir, and wait 30 minutes. If the water is cloudy,
filter it first and double the dose. You should smell a faint chlorine odour; this is how you
purify water with bleach when you cannot boil it.

```
1 litre  -> 2 drops
1 gallon -> 8 drops
```
