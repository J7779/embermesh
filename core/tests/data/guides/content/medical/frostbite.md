# Frostbite

Frostbite is freezing of the skin and the tissue under it. Fingers, toes, nose and ears are
affected first. Frostbitten skin looks white or greyish-yellow and feels hard or waxy.

Rewarm in water at 37–39 °C only if there is no risk of refreezing. Do not rub the area.
Hypothermia must be treated before frostbite.
