# Stopping Severe Bleeding

Severe bleeding can kill in minutes. Act fast.

## Direct pressure

1. Put on gloves if you have them.
2. Press hard on the wound with a clean cloth. **Do not lift it to look.**
3. If blood soaks through, add more cloth on top and keep pressing.

## When the bleeding won't stop

If firm pressure does not stop the bleeding from an arm or leg, apply a tourniquet 5–7 cm
above the wound (not over a joint) and tighten until the bleeding stops. Note the time.

| Step | Action |
|------|--------|
| 1 | Pressure |
| 2 | Packing |
| 3 | Tourniquet |
