# Snake Bites

Keep the person calm and still. Remove rings and watches before swelling starts.
Immobilise the bitten limb with a splint and keep it at heart level.

Do **not** cut the wound, suck out venom, or apply ice. Get the person to medical care.
A pressure bandage is recommended for some snake species (see [WHO guidance](https://www.who.int/)).
