# Licences and Credits

## The app

EmberMesh's own code is released under the **MIT License** (the file `LICENSE` in the source). You
may copy, change and pass it on freely, also to strangers, as long as the licence notice goes with it.

## The guide library is licensed separately

The guide library is **not** covered by the MIT License. It is collected from many sources, and
**each guide keeps its own licence**, shown at the bottom of every guide together with its source and
the credit line (attribution) the licence asks for. Only texts that anyone may copy, change and
redistribute for any purpose are included:

- **public-domain works** (for example US Government publications and old books),
- **CC0** (this operator manual is dedicated to the public domain under CC0),
- **CC BY** and **CC BY-SA** (for example Wikipedia and Wikibooks texts; CC BY-SA material stays under
  CC BY-SA when you pass it on or change it).

No "non-commercial", "no derivatives" or "all rights reserved" material is included. When you share
guides, keep their source, licence and attribution with them. The full explanation is in
`guides/LICENSES.md` in the source.

## Third-party components

A few components written by other people are included under their own licences: the cryptography
library, the sound-card library of the computer program, the QR-code scanner of the Android app, the
small search model inside the guide library ([Finding guides](library-search.md)), and parts of a QR
code generator. **Settings → About → Licences** lists each one with its version and licence; the source
file `THIRD_PARTY_LICENSES.md` has the details and the licence texts. Keep that file (or the texts it
points to) with any copy of the apps you pass on.
