## Considered and excluded

- WHO publications (mostly CC BY-NC-SA: non-commercial clause) — not used.
- Hesperian "Where There Is No Doctor" and related books — not openly licensed — not used.
- MedlinePlus A.D.A.M. medical encyclopedia — third-party copyrighted content on a .gov site — not used.
- FM 3-05.70 Survival (2002) — the available copy is marked "Distribution authorized to U.S. Government agencies and their contractors only" — not used.
- FM 21-10 Field Hygiene and Sanitation (2000) — openly releasable, but the only copy found has a broken font encoding (unreadable text layer); FM 4-25.12 and TC 4-02.3 used instead.
- FM 4-25.11 First Aid (2002) — openly releasable but superseded; its current successor ATP 4-02.11 (2026) is used instead.
- American Red Cross, NHS, Mayo Clinic and similar — copyrighted or not compatible — not used.
- SAMHSA tip-sheet PDFs (library.samhsa.gov) — refused automated download; not used.
