## Health and Sanitation Lessons (Africa) (Peace Corps ICE Reprint Series No. 27, 1978; ERIC ED243818)

- **Licence:** not established (earlier claim: Public Domain (US Government work, 17 U.S.C. 105))
- **Decision (2026-11-14):** removed before publication: licence not established. Author: adapted from lessons by Peace Corps Volunteers in Niger, translated for The Gambia. No contract is named, but the publication gives no evidence that the writer(s) were federal employees (Peace Corps Volunteers are not federal employees for copyright purposes), and neither the ERIC scan (full OCR text searched for "reproduc", "royalty", "public domain", "copyright") nor the ERIC record (https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED243818) carries a public-domain or reproduce-freely statement. No later Peace Corps edition carrying the reproduce-freely statement was found (files.peacecorps.gov searched; library URLs for the ICE number return 403).
- **Evidence:** Published by the US Peace Corps Information Collection and Exchange (a US Government agency); the document carries no copyright notice. ERIC record: https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED243818 (full text https://files.eric.ed.gov/fulltext/ED243818.pdf). Passages marked in the document as reprinted or adapted with permission from third parties were left out.
- **Downloaded from:** https://archive.org/details/micro_IA41153320_0234 (OCR text of the ERIC microfiche scan)
- **Attribution:** Adapted from lessons developed by Peace Corps Niger health Volunteers; translated by Peace Corps for The Gambia. Health and Sanitation Lessons (Africa). Peace Corps ICE Reprint Series No. 27, 1978. Public domain US Government publication; scan via ERIC (ED243818) and the Internet Archive.
- **Changes:** Text taken from optical character recognition of the microfiche scan and converted to Markdown; split into chapters; two-column pages put into reading order; illustrations not reproduced (captions kept); tables and worked calculations that could not be read reliably were left out and marked; front matter, order forms and back-cover address lists omitted.
- **Date:** 2026-11-13
- **Guides:** none (removed before publication: licence not established)
