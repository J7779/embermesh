> **Editor's note:** Canning methods and processing times in this historical text are not the tested times used today. Low-acid foods (vegetables, meat, poultry, fish, soups) must be processed in a pressure canner by current tested schedules or they can cause botulism; see the USDA Complete Guide to Home Canning elsewhere in this library.
