# ATP 4-41 Army Field Feeding and Class I Operations

ATP 4-41 describes how the Army feeds people in the field. Included here are the chapters on running a field kitchen (site selection, layout, sanitation, feeding methods and waste) and on water treatment and waste management for food service.

The publication carries the statement "DISTRIBUTION RESTRICTION. Approved for public release; distribution is unlimited." and, as a work of the US Government, is in the public domain. Illustrations are not reproduced; figure captions are kept as placeholders.
