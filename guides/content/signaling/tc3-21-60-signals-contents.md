# TC 3-21.60 Visual Signals

TC 3-21.60 Visual Signals (successor of FM 21-60) describes the Army's standard hand-and-arm, flag, panel, light and pyrotechnic signals for silent communication within a group, guiding vehicles and marshalling aircraft, and ground-to-air emergency codes.

The publication carries the statement "DISTRIBUTION RESTRICTION: Approved for public release; distribution is unlimited." and, as a work of the US Government, is in the public domain. Illustrations are not reproduced; figure captions are kept as placeholders.
