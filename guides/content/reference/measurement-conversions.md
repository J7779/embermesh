# Measurement Conversions (Metric and US Units)

Exact factors are marked with an asterisk (*). Others are rounded to four significant figures, which is more than enough for field work.

## Length

| From | To | Multiply by |
|---|---|---|
| inch (in) | centimeter (cm) | 2.54* |
| foot (ft) | meter (m) | 0.3048* |
| yard (yd) | meter (m) | 0.9144* |
| mile (mi, statute) | kilometer (km) | 1.609 |
| nautical mile (NM) | kilometer (km) | 1.852* |
| centimeter | inch | 0.3937 |
| meter | foot | 3.281 |
| meter | yard | 1.094 |
| kilometer | mile | 0.6214 |
| kilometer | nautical mile | 0.5400 |

- 1 nautical mile = 1 minute of latitude (approximately), so 1 degree of latitude is about 60 NM or 111 km.
- 1 fathom = 6 ft = 1.829 m.

## Area

| hectare | acre | 2.471 |

- 1 hectare = 10,000 m² (a square 100 m on a side). 1 acre = 43,560 ft² (about 63.6 m on a side).

## Temperature

- °C = (°F − 32) × 5 ⁄ 9
- °F = °C × 9 ⁄ 5 + 32
- K = °C + 273.15

| °C | °F | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| −40 | −40 | the two scales meet |
| −18 | 0 | home freezer temperature |
| 0 | 32 | water freezes |
| 4 | 40 | upper limit for refrigerated food |
| 37 | 98.6 | normal body temperature |
| 60 | 140 | lower limit for holding hot food |
| 100 | 212 | water boils at sea level |
| 116 | 240 | pressure canner at 10 lb (sea level) |

Water boils about 1 °C lower for every 300 m of altitude (about 1 °F per 500 ft).

## Angles and direction

- A full circle is 360 degrees; 1 degree = 60 minutes of arc; 1 minute = 60 seconds of arc.
- NATO military mils: 6,400 mils in a circle, so 1 degree ≈ 17.78 mils. At 1,000 m, 1 mil subtends about 1 m.
- The sun moves across the sky about 15 degrees per hour.

## Quick field estimates

- 1 meter ≈ 1 long stride; 1 km ≈ 600 to 700 of one person's double paces (count each time the same foot strikes) on level ground; calibrate your own pace count.
- 1 inch ≈ the width of an adult thumb at the knuckle. Measure your own hand span, foot length and arm span once against a ruler so you can use them as field rulers later.
- Arm's length: a fist held at arm's length spans about 10 degrees; a thumb width about 2 degrees; the spread of thumb and little finger about 20 to 25 degrees.
- 1 liter ≈ 1 US quart; 1 kilogram ≈ 2.2 pounds; 30 grams ≈ 1 ounce.
