# Sources for the `electronics` guides tagged `pdbooks`

Public-domain practical books added to this category. Each book was taken from a public-domain transcription, converted to plain Markdown and split into chapter files. Changes: transcription headers, footers, licence text, transcriber credits, tables of contents, indexes, page numbers and advertisements were removed; illustrations were not reproduced (captions are kept as bracketed notes); some very long chapters were split into parts and very short ones combined; a few books were shortened by leaving out non-practical chapters (noted below). Editor's notes were added at the top of chapters whose safety or medical advice is dated; the historical text itself was not rewritten.

## The Boy Electrician, by Alfred Powell Morgan (Lothrop, Lee & Shepard Co., Boston, 1913)

- **Licence:** Public Domain
- **Evidence:** Public domain in the United States (published before 1930).
- **Transcription:** Project Gutenberg eBook #63207 (https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/63207); Project Gutenberg header, footer, licence and name removed
- **Editor's note added to every chapter:** Early-1900s wiring practice (bare or cloth-insulated wire, no earthing, open fuses) does not meet modern safety codes. Low-voltage battery work is safe to try; anything connected to mains power should follow current electrical code.
- **Date:** 2026-11-13
- **Guides (1):**
  - `electronics/morgan-boy-electrician-01-introduction`
