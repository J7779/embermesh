# ATP 4-44/MCRP 3-40D.14 Water Support Operations

ATP 4-44 Water Support Operations covers how the Army and Marine Corps find, treat, store, test and distribute drinking water in the field. Included here are the practical parts: water treatment basics, water quality and safety, finding and developing water sources, cold-weather water support and the treatment processes.

The publication carries the statement "DISTRIBUTION RESTRICTION: Approved for public release; distribution is unlimited." and, as a work of the US Government, is in the public domain. Illustrations are not reproduced; figure captions are kept as placeholders.
