# FM 5-415 Fire-Fighting Operations

FM 5-415 is the Army engineer firefighters' manual. Included here are the chapters useful to anyone organizing fire protection for a community: fire prevention and firefighting water supply, rescue from crashed aircraft and vehicles, structural firefighting (size-up, forcible entry, ventilation, search and rescue in buildings and collapsed structures), fuel-site fire protection and hazardous-materials incidents.

The publication carries the statement "DISTRIBUTION RESTRICTION: Approved for public release; distribution is unlimited." and, as a work of the US Government, is in the public domain. Illustrations are not reproduced; figure captions are kept as placeholders.
