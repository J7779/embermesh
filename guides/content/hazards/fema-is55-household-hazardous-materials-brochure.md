##### Introduction

This course will introduce you to household hazardous materials and things you can do to protect yourself and your family from injury or death.

Even though you may not realize it, most American families have become dependent upon the daily use of chemical products in our homes. Many of these chemical products require special handling, storage, and disposal because they can be hazardous to our health and environment.

The danger from a chemical product depends greatly on the individuals using it. Chemicals are safe to use when people read the directions and use them cor rectly.

This course is completely Web-based and contains interactive activities. CD’s also are available by request.

You can take the course at your own pace from the comfort of your own home. It will take 1 to 3 hours to complete all the units and take the final exam.

##### How to enroll What will you learn?

To enroll in the course go to the U.S. Fire Administration’s Web site at www.usfa.fema.gov and click on “For the Fire Service/Virtual Campus.” Log on and select “IS- 55” from the course catalog to complete the course online. A certificate will be issued when you pass the final exam with a score of 75 percent or better.

In the first unit you will learn about basic chemical and physi cal properties, the ways chemicals enter your body, and the effects chemicals have on your body.

In the second and third units you will learn about cleaning products, lead, mercury, gaso line, compressed gases, carbon monoxide, medical supplies, chlorine, pesticides, and much, much more.

The last two units cover labels, personal protective equipment, proper disposal methods, preven tive measures to take before natu ral disasters occur, and less toxic alternatives.
