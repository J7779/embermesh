# FM 55-501 Marine Crewman's Handbook

FM 55-501 is the handbook for Army watercraft crews. Included here are the chapters that complement the Coast Guard seamanship manual: tides and currents, shipboard weather observation, marine radio and visual signaling (flags, flashing light, distress signals), marine emergencies and abandoning ship, marlinespike seamanship with wire rope, deck maintenance (tools, rust removal and painting), blocks and tackles, anchors and ground tackle, and damage control (shoring and plugging).

The publication carries the statement "DISTRIBUTION RESTRICTION: Approved for public release; distribution is unlimited." and, as a work of the US Government, is in the public domain. Illustrations are not reproduced; figure captions are kept as placeholders.
