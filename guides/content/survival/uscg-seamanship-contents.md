# COMDTINST M16114.5C Boat Crew Seamanship Manual

The US Coast Guard Boat Crew Seamanship Manual is the classic training text for small-boat crews of the Coast Guard and Coast Guard Auxiliary. Included here are its practical chapters: crew health factors (fatigue, seasickness, fumes, cold and heat), first aid, survival equipment, life rafts and capsizing, knots, splices and line handling, boat construction and troubleshooting, stability, boat handling, marine radio procedure, weather and oceanography, aids to navigation, coastal navigation and piloting, search and rescue, person-in-the-water recovery and water survival, towing, fire fighting and damage control, helicopter operations and heavy-weather and surf operations. Coast Guard organization and policy chapters (1, 2 and 4) are not included.

The publication carries the statement "Internet Release Authorized." and, as a work of the US Government, is in the public domain. Illustrations are not reproduced; figure captions are kept as placeholders.
