# ATP 3-21.18 Foot Marches

ATP 3-21.18 Foot Marches is the Army's manual on moving people on foot with loads: march planning, pace, halts, water and heat discipline, load carrying, posture, training, foot care and march injuries. The chapter on tactical march execution is not included.

The publication carries the statement "DISTRIBUTION RESTRICTION: Approved for public release: distribution is unlimited." and, as a work of the US Government, is in the public domain. Illustrations are not reproduced; figure captions are kept as placeholders.
