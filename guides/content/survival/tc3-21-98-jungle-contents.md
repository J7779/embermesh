# TC 3-21.98 Jungle Handbook

The Jungle Handbook (TC 3-21.98) covers living and moving in tropical jungles. Included here are the chapters useful outside combat: jungle environment, movement and navigation, tracking, jungle mobility with ropes, boats and river crossings, radio communications in the jungle, first aid and tropical health, waterborne diseases and a collection of practical jungle tips.

The publication carries the statement "DISTRIBUTION RESTRICTION: Approved for public release; distribution is unlimited." and, as a work of the US Government, is in the public domain. Illustrations are not reproduced; figure captions are kept as placeholders.
