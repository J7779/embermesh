# TC 3-21.76 Ranger Handbook

The Ranger Handbook (TC 3-21.76) is the pocket reference of the US Army Ranger course. Only its chapters with civilian survival value are included here: field radio communications and antennas, waterborne operations (rope bridges, poncho rafts, small boats and river movement) and casualty care with field sanitation. Tactical, weapons and demolition chapters were deliberately left out.

The publication carries the statement "DISTRIBUTION RESTRICTION: Approved for public release; distribution is unlimited." and, as a work of the US Government, is in the public domain. Illustrations are not reproduced; figure captions are kept as placeholders.
