# TC 21-3 Soldier's Handbook for Individual Operations and Survival in Cold-Weather Areas

Training Circular 21-3 is the Army's pocket handbook for living, moving and surviving in snow and extreme cold: cold-weather clothing and layering, sleeping systems, eating and drinking in the cold, cold injuries and carbon-monoxide poisoning, tent life and stoves, snowshoes, skis and sleds, ice crossing, navigation in snow, improvised shelters, food and knots.

The publication carries the statement "DISTRIBUTION RESTRICTION: Approved for public release; distribution is unlimited." and, as a work of the US Government, is in the public domain. Illustrations are not reproduced; figure captions are kept as placeholders.
