# TC 3-97.61 Military Mountaineering

Training Circular 3-97.61 is the Army's complete mountaineering manual: mountain terrain and weather, altitude illness, nutrition and sustainment, avalanche safety and rescue, walking, scrambling and climbing on rock, snow and ice, navigation, knots and rope care, anchors, belays, fixed ropes and rappelling, hauling and lowering systems, stream crossing, glacier travel and crevasse rescue, mountain rescue and casualty evacuation, and rope bridges.

The publication carries the statement "DISTRIBUTION RESTRICTION: Approved for public release; distribution is unlimited." and, as a work of the US Government, is in the public domain. Illustrations are not reproduced; figure captions are kept as placeholders.
