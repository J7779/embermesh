# ATP 3-34.45/MCRP 3-40D.17 Electric Power Generation and Distribution

ATP 3-34.45 covers how the military generates and distributes electric power in the field, from soldier-portable batteries and solar sets to generators and power grids. Included here are the chapters on power basics, tactical and alternative power, power planning and load management, electrical inspection, electrical safety and international wiring and plug standards.

The publication carries the statement "DISTRIBUTION RESTRICTION: Approved for public release; distribution is unlimited." and, as a work of the US Government, is in the public domain. Illustrations are not reproduced; figure captions are kept as placeholders.
