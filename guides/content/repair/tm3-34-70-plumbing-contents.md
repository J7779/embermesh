# TM 3-34.70/MCRP 3-17.7E Plumbing, Pipe Fitting, and Sewerage

TM 3-34.70 (successor of FM 3-34.471 and FM 5-420) is the Army and Marine Corps plumbing manual: water supply and distribution piping, disinfecting new lines, sewers, septic tanks and leach fields, wastewater, latrines and solid waste in the field, heating systems, repairs of leaks, frozen pipes and stoppages, fixtures, water heaters, pipe and fittings of every material, valves and faucets, stacks, traps and vents, gas and air piping, centrifugal pumps and pipe insulation.

The publication carries the statement "DISTRIBUTION RESTRICTION: Approved for public release; distribution is unlimited." and, as a work of the US Government, is in the public domain. Illustrations are not reproduced; figure captions are kept as placeholders.
