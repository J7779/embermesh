# ATP 4-31/MCRP 3-40E.1 Ground Equipment Battle Damage Assessment, Repair, and Recovery

ATP 4-31 covers getting stuck or damaged vehicles moving again: improvised (nonstandard) repairs, self-recovery and towing, winching, rigging with ropes, chains, slings and pulleys, anchors and mechanical advantage, and the hand signals and safety rules for recovery work.

The publication carries the statement "DISTRIBUTION RESTRICTION. Approved for public release; distribution is unlimited." and, as a work of the US Government, is in the public domain. Illustrations are not reproduced; figure captions are kept as placeholders.
