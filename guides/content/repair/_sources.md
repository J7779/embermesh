## Considered but excluded

- Nuclear War Survival Skills (C. Kearny): the 1987 edition's notice permits reproduction only 'full-scale' with the notice; it does not clearly permit modification, and the 1979 ORNL-5037 report is a contractor work without an explicit public-domain statement. Excluded under the 'when in doubt, leave it out' rule.
- USGS Volcanic Ash Impacts site: co-authored with non-US-Government institutions (GNS Science, University of Canterbury, Massey University), so not clearly public domain. Excluded.
- ARRL and ham-club publications: copyrighted. Not used.
- FEMA / Ready.gov / CDC (www.cdc.gov) / NRC / OSHA / CPSC pages: public domain, but their servers refused automated downloads (HTTP 403), so they were not used.
