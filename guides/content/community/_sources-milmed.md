# Sources for the `community` category — military and US Government medicine (milmed)

Guides listed here come from US Government works (public domain, 17 U.S.C. 105). Licences were verified on 2026-11-13; each guide's own front matter repeats its source, licence and attribution.

Licence decision for all chapters below: the Textbooks of Military Medicine are published by the Office of The Surgeon General, US Army, through the Borden Institute. Their copyright page says "Certain parts of this publication pertain to copyright restrictions", which covers material credited to outside copyright holders. Only chapters whose every author is identified in the book (chapter title page or the volume's contributor list) as a US Government employee at the time of writing (active-duty military officer, or civilian staff of a federal agency such as USARIEM, Naval Health Research Center, USUHS or USDA) were used; such text is a US Government work (17 U.S.C. 105). Chapters with any university, private-sector, retired or foreign-government author were excluded. Within the chapters used, every figure, table or exhibit credited as "reprinted/adapted with permission" or taken from a commercial publisher was left out and the omission is noted in the guide. Photographs, graphs and literature references were not reproduced.
