## Homemaking Handbook for Village Workers in Many Countries (US Department of Agriculture Extension Service with the Agency for International Development; Peace Corps ICE Reprint R-39, 1981; ERIC ED307465)

- **Licence:** not established (earlier claim: Public Domain (US Government work, 17 U.S.C. 105))
- **Decision (2026-11-14):** removed before publication: licence not established. Author: USDA Extension Service with AID, with outside contributors. The foreword says "Material was also adapted from the published works of the following authorities" (FAO, University of the West Indies and Cornell authors) and credits outside compilers and consultants, without marking which passages; so the text cannot be shown to be wholly a US Government work, and the publication carries no public-domain or reproduce-freely statement. No later Peace Corps edition carrying the reproduce-freely statement was found (files.peacecorps.gov searched; library URLs for the ICE number return 403).
- **Evidence:** Published by the US Peace Corps Information Collection and Exchange (a US Government agency) reprinting a US Department of Agriculture / US Government publication; the document carries no copyright notice. ERIC record: https://eric.ed.gov/?id=ED307465 (full text https://files.eric.ed.gov/fulltext/ED307465.pdf). Passages marked in the document as reprinted or adapted with permission from third parties were left out.
- **Downloaded from:** https://archive.org/details/micro_IA41153301_0060 (OCR text of the ERIC microfiche scan)
- **Attribution:** Extension Service, US Department of Agriculture, in cooperation with the Agency for International Development. Homemaking Handbook for Village Workers in Many Countries. US Department of Agriculture Extension Service with the Agency for International Development; Peace Corps ICE Reprint R-39, 1981. Public domain US Government publication; scan via ERIC (ED307465) and the Internet Archive.
- **Changes:** Text taken from optical character recognition of the microfiche scan and converted to Markdown; split into chapters; two-column pages put into reading order; illustrations not reproduced (captions kept); tables and worked calculations that could not be read reliably were left out and marked; front matter, order forms and back-cover address lists omitted.
- **Date:** 2026-11-13
- **Guides:** none (removed before publication: licence not established)
