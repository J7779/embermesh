# Sources for the `radio` guides added by the fieldman contributor

US military and uniformed-service field and technical manuals. Each was used only because its own distribution statement
permits public release (quoted below) and, as a work of the US Government (17 U.S.C. 105), it is in the public domain.
Retrieved 2026-11-13. Converted from the PDF text layer to Markdown; running heads, page numbers and figure labels were
removed, tables rebuilt, illustrations not reproduced (captions kept as placeholders). Chapters with no civilian survival
use (weapons, tactics, organization, administration) were left out.

## Considered but excluded (fieldman)

- **AFR 64-4 Vol I, Search and Rescue Survival Training (USAF, 15 July 1985)** — the cover carries no public-release statement (only "Distribution: F") and states "This publication includes copyrighted material." The copyrighted parts are not identified, so the text cannot be used.
- **ATP 3-50.21 Survival (US Army, 2018)** — although marked "Approved for public release", it also carries a "DESTRUCTION NOTICE: Destroy by any means that will prevent disclosure of contents or reconstruction of the document", which this project treats as excluded.
- **ATP 3-18.13 Special Forces Use of Pack Animals (2024) and other armypubs "DR_c" titles** — distribution-restricted (CAC login required); not used.
- **AFMAN 64-5 Aircrew Survival** — DTIC refused automated download (HTTP 403); no official alternative host found, so skipped.
- **USCG Boat Crew Seamanship Manual, chapter 7 splicing illustrations** — the double-braid splicing steps are copyright Samson Ocean Systems and reprinted by permission; they are images in the PDF and were not reproduced.
- Tactical, weapons, demolition and organizational chapters of the manuals above were omitted on purpose (no civilian survival use).
- **FM 5-34 Engineer Field Data (2003 edition)** — "Distribution is authorized to US Government agencies only"; not used.
- **FM 5-484 Multiservice Procedures for Well-Drilling Operations (1994)** and **TM 3-34.46 Theater of Operations Electrical Systems (2013)** — approved for public release, but each states that it contains copyrighted material used with permission (drilling-industry sources; "Basic Home Wiring", Sunset Publishing) without marking which passages; not used.
- **FM 5-420 Plumbing and Pipefitting (1993)** and **FM 5-103 Survivability (1985)** — the available copies show no distribution statement; not used (plumbing is covered by the public-release TM 3-34.70).
- **FM 5-426 Carpentry (1995)** and **FM 5-428 Concrete and Masonry (1998)** — public release, but the only available copies are partly scanned images or have a broken text layer, so a faithful conversion was not possible in this pass.
- **Marine Corps publications on marines.mil** — the site refused automated download (HTTP 403); skipped.
